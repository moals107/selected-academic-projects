# Koch Snowflake — Mathematical Visualization

**Python · Jupyter · NumPy · Matplotlib**

This project explores the iterative construction of the **Koch snowflake** and visualizes how the geometric structure develops over successive iterations.

## What I worked on

- generation of Koch-snowflake iterations
- visualization of the construction process
- comparison of iterations 0–6
- analysis of perimeter growth
- analysis of area convergence
- mathematical interpretation of the limiting behavior

## Mathematical Background

For each iteration, every line segment is replaced by four segments of one third of the original length.

This leads to a perimeter that grows without bound, while the enclosed area converges to a finite value.

The project combined the mathematical derivation with Python-based numerical visualization.
