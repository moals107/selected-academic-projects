# JupiGuide — Digital Innovation Concept

**Product Concept · AI/AR · MVP · User Experience**

JupiGuide was a team project developed in the context of digital innovation. The idea was to design a personalized city-guide experience that combines historical information, route recommendations and location-based media.

## Concept

The application concept included:

- personalized city tours
- interactive map-based navigation
- historical images and contextual information
- audio-based content
- AI-supported recommendations
- augmented-reality-oriented interaction concepts
- an MVP-oriented product approach

## My Takeaways

The project gave me practical exposure to:

- translating a user problem into a product concept
- structuring a value proposition
- defining an MVP
- thinking about user journeys and product usability
- evaluating how AI and location-based technologies could support a digital product

## Context

This was a **concept and innovation project**, not a production-ready implementation. The focus was on product thinking, problem framing and digital innovation.
